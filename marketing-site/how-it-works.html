01

Submit your project

Upload your capstone project — write-up, media, and links — in a few minutes. No cost, no commitment beyond that.

Our team reviews what you submit for completeness and authenticity before it goes anywhere near a professional.

02

Get reviewed & listed

Once approved, your project goes live under the right category — engineering, health, fintech, and more.

Most submissions are reviewed and listed within a few days.

03

Get discovered

Industry professionals browse by category and can reach out directly the moment your project catches their eye.

There's no feed to game and no algorithm to chase — just real work, organized so it's easy to find.

04

Connect & collaborate

Message professionals directly — mentorship, internships, investment, or just advice.

You're always in control of what happens next. Nothing moves without your say-so.
